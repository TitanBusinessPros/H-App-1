How to Use the Halloween Flyer Builder

  • Edit the text fields to customize your flyer content
  • Change colors and themes to match your Halloween style
  • Add extra elements like text and Halloween graphics
  • Drag elements around the flyer to position them
  • Click on elements to select them (a red delete button will appear)
  • Click the delete button or use the "Delete Selected" button to remove elements
  • When you're happy with your design, click "Export as PNG" to download an 11 × 8.5 inch landscape flyer at 300 DPI
